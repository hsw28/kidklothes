Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 24, 2026

Layette Out is designed as a local-first app. Your data is stored on your device in a local database, and we do not require an account to use the app.

What we collect

We do not collect your closet, wishlist, or child profile data on our servers.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use your data for advertising purposes.

We use analytics tools to understand how Layette Out is used and to improve the app.

This may include information about how you interact with the app, such as screens viewed, actions taken, and general usage patterns. This data helps us identify bugs, improve features, and enhance the overall user experience.

We do not use this information to personally identify you. Analytics data is collected in an aggregated or anonymous manner and is not linked to your identity.

Purchases and Subscriptions

If you choose to purchase a subscription or one-time upgrade, payment is processed securely through Apple. We do not have access to your full payment details.

We may receive information about your purchase status, such as whether you have an active subscription, in order to unlock features within the app.

Photos

If you choose photos for items or profiles, those photos are stored locally on your device and remain under your control.

Outbound links

If you save or open wishlist links to external stores, you are leaving the app and visiting a third-party website. Those sites have their own privacy policies and practices.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@layetteout.com.
