Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 24, 2026

Layette Out is designed as a local-first app. Your data is stored on your device in a local database, and we do not require an account to use the app.

What we collect

We do not collect your closet, wishlist, or child profile data on our servers. We do not use tracking analytics, and we do not sell personal data.

Photos

If you choose photos for items or profiles, those photos are stored locally on your device and remain under your control.

Outbound links

If you save or open wishlist links to external stores, you are leaving the app and visiting a third-party website. Those sites have their own privacy policies and practices.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@layetteout.com.
